Privacy & Cookies

009 Couture

What we collect

When you browse our store we collect basic, non-identifying usage data — pages viewed, clicks, device type and the site that referred you — to understand how the store is used and improve it. We do not collect this until you press Accept on the cookie banner.

Analytics

We use PostHog (EU-hosted) as our analytics provider. It sets cookies in your browser to recognise return visits and measure the shopping journey (for example: product views, add-to-cart, checkout). Data is stored in the European Union.

Orders

When you place an order we store the details you provide (name, contact, delivery address and the items ordered) solely to process and deliver your order. Payment is handled by our payment provider; we do not store card or wallet credentials.

Your choice

You can Decline analytics cookies at any time — the store works fully without them. To change your choice, clear this site’s data in your browser and reload.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us through the store’s contact channel and we’ll help.
